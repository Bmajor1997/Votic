Delete your Votic account

In Votic, open Account, choose Delete account, and complete both confirmations. If asked, sign out and sign back in first.

Request deletion without the app

Fill in the account deletion request form. Use the email address on your Votic account. We must verify account ownership before completing a request.

Do not send your password, authentication tokens, private documents, or payment details.

You can also request deletion without the app or external form by emailing votic.support@gmail.com from your account email address with the subject "Votic account deletion". We may verify control of the account email or ask you to sign in. We do not ask for your password. See our Privacy Policy for support and form processing, retention and privacy-request rights.

What is removed

Deletion removes your Votic sign-in, private AI reports and account-linked backend records, and requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer profile. The updated app erases this account's local library, notes, activity, preferences, pronunciations and temporary imports. Other updated devices clear their local copies when they next connect and detect deletion; offline devices cannot be erased remotely while disconnected.

Usage rows with account and provider links removed are deleted when their original request timestamp is more than 90 days old; removal of links does not guarantee anonymity. A hash of the account identifier is retained to prevent deleted sessions from regaining access and late purchase events from recreating data; it currently has no automatic expiry. See the Privacy Policy for retention exceptions, backups and provider records.

Subscriptions are separate

Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Manage cancellation and refunds through the store where you purchased.

Manage Google Play subscriptions, or follow Apple cancellation instructions for an Apple purchase. Contact support for help; your mandatory refund and consumer rights remain available.

If deletion is interrupted, reopen Account and retry. Some data may already have been erased; Votic does not report success until the requested steps finish.
