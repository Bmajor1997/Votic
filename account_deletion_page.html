Delete your Votic account

In Votic, open Account, choose Delete account, and complete both confirmations. If asked, sign out and sign back in first.

Request deletion without the app

Email Votic to request account deletion. Send the request from your registered email address if possible. We must verify account ownership before completing a request.

Do not send your password, authentication tokens, private documents, or payment details.

What is removed

Deletion removes your Votic sign-in, private AI reports and account-linked backend records, and requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer profile. The updated app erases this account's local library, notes, activity, preferences, pronunciations and temporary imports. Other updated devices clear their local copies when they next connect and detect deletion; offline devices cannot be erased remotely while disconnected.

Anonymous spending totals are retained for up to 90 days for financial controls. A hash of the account identifier is retained to prevent deleted sessions from regaining access. Service-provider security or legal retention and backups follow the published Privacy Policy and provider terms.

Subscriptions are separate

Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Manage cancellation and refunds through the store where you purchased.

If deletion is interrupted, reopen Account and retry. Some data may already have been erased; Votic does not report success until the requested steps finish.
