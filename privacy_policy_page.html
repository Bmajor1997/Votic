Votic Privacy Policy

Revision prepared: October 31, 2026. Effective when published following any required notice.

Votic is an app for reading documents, listening to them read aloud, and asking questions about them. This privacy policy applies to the Votic app on platforms where it is offered, the Votic server, and our supporting websites, account-deletion forms and support correspondence.

Who we are

Votic is developed and run by Benjamin Major, an individual developer based in Georgia, United States ("Votic", "we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, contact votic.support@gmail.com. This policy explains what information Votic handles, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we receive your email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider shares your name and email address with Votic, and Google may also share your profile photo. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which assigns you an account identifier. If you sign in with Google using the same email address as an existing email-and-password account that hasn't been verified, Firebase may switch that account to Google sign-in; you can set a password again with "Forgot password?".

Subscription information. If you subscribe to Votic Plus, Google Play or the App Store processes your payment. We never see your card details. RevenueCat tells us whether your subscription is active, its plan and its renewal status, linked to your Votic account identifier.

Device and network information. When the app connects to our services, some technical information is processed automatically:

  • The Votic server receives your IP address with each request. It uses it briefly to limit abuse (for example, too many requests at once) and does not store it in its application database. Hosting and network services may separately process connection and access logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Google Firebase Authentication processes your IP address and device information, such as the app version and operating system, to sign you in and protect accounts from misuse.
  • The RevenueCat software in the app processes your Votic account identifier, purchase records, app version, device and operating system details, and IP address to check and restore your subscription.

Votic does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or browsing history.

Documents you import. Your library, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and preferences are stored on your device. Some imports are processed on your device. When server processing is needed, the file or webpage address is sent to the Votic server to extract readable text and return it to your device. The backend does not save imported files or extracted text to its database. Scanned pages and images may also be sent to OpenAI for text recognition.

AI requests. When you use Ask Votic, summaries, voice questions or reading scanned pages, the passage, question or image needed for that request is sent through the Votic server to OpenAI to produce the answer. We record the cost and size of each request against your monthly allowance, not its content. OpenAI does not use API inputs and outputs for model training by default. Its abuse-monitoring systems may retain request content for up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its API data controls. This provider retention is separate from Votic's own database.

AI request content can include your question, relevant document sections, recent conversation context, included notes, scan images or scanned-PDF uploads. For a short document, the context may include the whole document. Avoid sending content you do not want processed by OpenAI. Device camera or microphone permission is separate from permission to share content with an AI provider. Provider retention depends on the endpoint and applicable configuration; OpenAI's transcription endpoint has different retention rules from document-answer requests. Disabling response storage does not by itself eliminate abuse-monitoring or exceptional safety or legal retention.

Reports and feedback you send. If you report an AI answer or send feedback, we store what you send, linked to your account identifier, so we can review it. Only the Votic administrator can read it. Reported answers are deleted after 30 days; feature requests and bug reports are retained for up to 365 days, unless removed earlier or when you delete your account. Please don't include passwords, payment details or private document content in feedback.

Optional failure counts. If you turn on "Share failure counts" in Account settings, the app sends a daily count of screen errors, with no document text, notes, audio or purchase data. Counts are kept for up to 30 days. This is off by default.

Support and deletion requests. When you email support or submit an account-deletion request, we process your email address, message, relevant account details and the information needed to verify ownership and resolve the request. Our support address uses Gmail. The linked external deletion form is hosted by Framer; visiting it may also disclose technical connection information to that provider. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, private documents or payment-card details. You can email support directly instead of using the external form.

Votic uses your phone's text-to-speech service for listening; Votic does not send text to OpenAI to generate spoken audio. Voice availability and any network processing by your selected speech engine depend on that engine and your device settings.

Sensitive documents

Please avoid importing highly sensitive documents, such as medical records, financial or bank statements, government ID documents, or documents containing passwords or other people's private information. Votic doesn't need them to work. If you do import them, they are handled exactly as described in this policy. Your library stays on your device, but any part you send for server processing or an AI feature (importing, scanning, Ask Votic, summaries or voice questions) is processed by the Votic server and, for AI features, by OpenAI.

Permissions, microphone and camera

Votic asks for device permissions only when you use a feature that needs them, and every permission is optional. Reading, listening and notes work without any of them.

  • Camera: asked when you scan a page. The photo is used to recognize the page's text. Scan photos are kept on your device until you finish or discard the scan.
  • Microphone: asked only if voice questions are available and you choose to record one. The recording is sent through the Votic server to OpenAI to turn it into text. The Votic server does not save your recording; it is used only for that request, subject to OpenAI's retention described above.
  • Notifications: used to show playback controls while Votic reads aloud in the background.

You can turn any permission off at any time in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings › Apps › Votic › Permissions). If you turn one off, only the feature that needs it stops working.

Exported backups

You can export your library to a backup file using the app's sharing tools. Exported backups are not encrypted by Votic. You control where you save or share them, and account deletion does not remove copies saved outside the app.

Local app data also includes reading and listening activity, conversation history, scan drafts, temporary import copies, preferences and remembered sign-in details where enabled. Deleted library items may remain recoverable in Recently Deleted for 30 days unless permanently deleted sooner. Votic does not provide automatic library cloud sync. Device operating-system backup or a selected speech engine may process data separately under your device settings and that provider's terms. Uninstalling the app or losing a device can cause loss of local data; exported or device backups can retain separate copies. Device access, widgets and playback controls can expose content to someone using or viewing your device.

How we use information

We use the information above only to run Votic: to sign you in, provide reading, listening and AI features, check your subscription and AI allowance, prevent abuse, fix problems, and respond to your feedback and requests.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your personal information, show ads, or use advertising trackers. We don't use your documents to train AI models. Because Votic doesn't track you across other apps or websites, there are no cross-site tracking or "Do Not Track" settings to change.

Who we share information with

We share information only with these service providers, and only what each needs to do its job: Google Firebase (sign-in), RevenueCat (subscription status), OpenAI (AI answers, text recognition and voice transcription), Google Cloud (hosting the Votic server, in the United States), and Google Play or the App Store (payments). These providers process data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of users or the service. Data may be processed outside your state or country.

Google Gmail also processes support correspondence, and Framer hosts the external account-deletion form linked from our deletion page. Your chosen device speech, backup and sharing services process information when you use them. Their independent services are also subject to their privacy policies. Contact us for information about providers relevant to a request.

How long we keep information

Account-linked usage metadata, including request times, feature and model names, token counts and estimated costs, is retained while your account exists to enforce usage and spending controls. When you delete your account, the backend removes reports, feedback and credit data and removes account and provider links from usage rows. Those de-identified usage rows are deleted when they are more than 90 days old. A hash of your account identifier is retained to block deleted sessions and prevent late purchase events from recreating credit data. Optional failure counts are retained for up to 30 days. IP addresses used to prevent abuse are not stored in Votic's database. Provider security, transaction and legal records may be retained under the providers' policies.

Removing direct identifiers or hashing an identifier does not necessarily make a record anonymous. The account-identifier hash currently has no automatic expiry. Its purpose is to reject deleted sessions and late purchase events; contact us about retention or deletion requests concerning that record. Support correspondence, request-verification records, access logs and operator-controlled backups are separate from the live application database. Retention depends on resolving the request, maintaining necessary security or transaction evidence, applicable legal obligations and backup rotation. Account deletion may not immediately remove separately held backup or provider copies, and does not erase records a provider must independently retain.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in two ways:

  • In the app: Settings › Account & Membership › Delete account.
  • On the web, without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Deletion removes your sign-in, requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer profile, and removes the account data described above, subject to the retention exceptions in this policy. The updated app erases the account's local data on the device completing deletion. Other updated devices clear their local account data when they next connect and detect deletion; disconnected devices cannot be erased remotely. External backup copies remain under your control. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription; cancel it in the store.

To delete some of your data without deleting your account, for example a feedback report, email votic.support@gmail.com. Documents and notes on your device can be deleted at any time in the app.

Your privacy choices and requests

Email votic.support@gmail.com to request access to or a copy of personal information we hold, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy rights. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, an authorized agent and an appeal of a refused request. We do not require you to give up mandatory rights to use this channel and do not discriminate for exercising applicable privacy rights. A local library export does not include server-held account or usage records.

Describe the request and the email address associated with your account. We may verify ownership through sign-in or control of that email and, for an authorized agent, appropriate evidence of authority. We will request only information reasonably needed to verify and handle the request; do not send passwords or identity documents unless we explain why additional evidence is necessary and provide an appropriate method. We respond within the deadlines required by applicable law, explain any permitted extension or refusal, and describe an applicable appeal route. You may also complain to the privacy regulator with authority over your situation.

You can disable optional failure counts in Settings, revoke device permissions in your device settings and stop using an optional feature. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us or using the relevant control; withdrawal does not undo lawful processing already completed. Information necessary to provide a requested feature may still be needed if you continue using that feature.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • Connections between the app and the Votic server use HTTPS encryption.
  • The server checks your sign-in for every account-specific request, and verifies subscriptions with RevenueCat rather than trusting the app.
  • Access to the server and its private records is limited to the operator.
  • On your device, each account's library is kept separately.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

Children

Votic is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

We will explain material changes and when they take effect before applying them where required. We will obtain fresh consent when required for a new use of previously collected information; continued app use is not a substitute for that consent.
