Votic Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Votic is an app for reading documents, listening to them read aloud, and asking questions about them. It is run by Benjamin Major ("Votic", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information Votic handles, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we receive your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google or Apple. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

Subscription information. If you subscribe to Votic Plus, Google Play or the App Store processes your payment. We never see your card details. RevenueCat tells us whether your subscription is active, its plan and its renewal status, linked to your Votic account identifier.

Documents you import. Your library, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and preferences are stored on your device. When you import a file or webpage, it is sent to the Votic server to extract its text, then returned to your device. The server does not keep a copy of the file or its text after the import finishes.

AI requests. When you use Ask Votic, summaries, voice questions or reading scanned pages, the passage, question or image needed for that request is sent through the Votic server to OpenAI to produce the answer. We record the cost and size of each request against your monthly allowance, not its content. OpenAI processes this data under its API terms, which do not allow using it to train its models.

Reports and feedback you send. If you report an AI answer or send feedback, we store what you send so we can review it. Reported answers are deleted after 30 days.

Optional failure counts. If you turn on "Share failure counts" in Account settings, the app sends a daily count of screen errors, with no document text, notes, audio or purchase data. Counts are kept for up to 30 days. This is off by default.

Text-to-speech uses your phone's own voices, so listening happens on your device.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information, show ads, or use advertising trackers. We don't use your documents to train AI models.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (sign-in), RevenueCat (subscription status), OpenAI (AI answers), Google Cloud (hosting the Votic server), and Google Play or the App Store (payments). Each processes data only to provide its service to Votic.

How long we keep information

Account and subscription records are kept while your account exists. Anonymous AI cost totals are kept for up to 90 days for spending controls. Server backups, if any, expire on a rolling schedule and are not used to restore deleted accounts.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Account › Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your sign-in, your server-side records and your RevenueCat customer profile, and erases the app's local data on your device. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription; cancel it in the store.

Security

Connections to the Votic server use HTTPS. Access to the server and its private records is limited to the operator.

Children

Votic is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
