Votic Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Votic is an app for reading documents, listening to them read aloud, and asking questions about them. It is run by Benjamin Major ("Votic", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information Votic handles, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account we receive your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google or Apple. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

Subscription information. If you subscribe to Votic Plus, Google Play or the App Store processes your payment. We never see your card details. RevenueCat tells us whether your subscription is active, its plan and its renewal status, linked to your Votic account identifier.

Documents you import. Your library, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and preferences are stored on your device. Some imports are processed on your device. When server processing is needed, the file or webpage address is sent to the Votic server to extract readable text and return it to your device. The backend does not save imported files or extracted text to its database. Scanned pages and images may also be sent to OpenAI for text recognition.

AI requests. When you use Ask Votic, summaries, voice questions or reading scanned pages, the passage, question or image needed for that request is sent through the Votic server to OpenAI to produce the answer. We record the cost and size of each request against your monthly allowance, not its content. OpenAI does not use API inputs and outputs for model training by default. Its abuse-monitoring systems may retain request content for up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its API data controls. This provider retention is separate from Votic's own database.

Reports and feedback you send. If you report an AI answer or send feedback, we store what you send so we can review it. Reported answers are deleted after 30 days; feature requests and bug reports are retained for up to 365 days, unless removed earlier or when you delete your account.

Optional failure counts. If you turn on "Share failure counts" in Account settings, the app sends a daily count of screen errors, with no document text, notes, audio or purchase data. Counts are kept for up to 30 days. This is off by default.

Votic uses your phone's text-to-speech service for listening; Votic does not send text to OpenAI to generate spoken audio. Voice availability and any network processing by your selected speech engine depend on that engine and your device settings.

Camera, microphone and exported backups

If you choose to scan a page, Votic requests camera access and processes the resulting image for text recognition. If voice questions are enabled and you choose to record one, the recording is sent to the backend and OpenAI for transcription. These actions require the relevant device permissions.

You can export your library to a backup file using the app's sharing tools. Exported backups are not encrypted by Votic. You control where you save or share them, and account deletion does not remove copies saved outside the app.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information, show ads, or use advertising trackers. We don't use your documents to train AI models.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (sign-in), RevenueCat (subscription status), OpenAI (AI answers), Google Cloud (hosting the Votic server), and Google Play or the App Store (payments). These providers process data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. Data may be processed outside your state or country.

How long we keep information

Account-linked usage metadata, including request times, feature and model names, token counts and estimated costs, is retained while your account exists to enforce usage and spending controls. When you delete your account, the backend removes reports, feedback and credit data and removes account and provider links from usage rows. Those de-identified usage rows are deleted when they are more than 90 days old. A hash of your account identifier is retained to block deleted sessions and prevent late purchase events from recreating credit data. Optional failure counts are retained for up to 30 days. Provider security, transaction and legal records may be retained under the providers' policies.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Account › Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your sign-in, requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer profile, and removes the account data described above, subject to the retention exceptions in this policy. The updated app erases the account's local data on the device completing deletion. Other updated devices clear their local account data when they next connect and detect deletion; disconnected devices cannot be erased remotely. External backup copies remain under your control. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription; cancel it in the store.

Security

Connections to the Votic server use HTTPS. Access to the server and its private records is limited to the operator.

Children

Votic is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
