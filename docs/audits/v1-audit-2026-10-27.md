### 4. Needs review

- Review the additive `credit_refund_states` SQLite migration and deployment on a staging copy before production. Backups must be taken privately and restored with the documented deletion/spend reconciliation.
- Existing deployments cannot recover previously dropped refund events from this new table. Reconcile historical one-time purchases/refunds with RevenueCat or replay verified events before launch. Do not fabricate ordering timestamps.
- Review equal-timestamp handling (revocation wins), RevenueCat alias/transfer behavior and account ownership during purchase/restore. Events whose account/product differs from the stored grant do not mutate that grant.
- Confirm Firebase project, RevenueCat entitlement, Google Play package/base plans, backend persistence, spending limits and single-ledger deployment agree with V1 policy.
- Review published legal disclosures for AI processing, optional diagnostics, cancellation/refunds, retention and readable portable backups. Policy links alone do not prove adequate legal content. No legal approval is claimed.
- Verify repository protections and reviewer requirements in authorized GitHub settings. Listing branches and passing tests do not establish protection against direct pushes.

### 5. Needs a second glance

- Physical Android acceptance: TalkBack/large text/reduced motion, Home layout, notification playback, audio interruption/headphone removal, process termination and storage pressure.
- Live store edge cases: pending purchase, grace/expiration, offline recovery, monthly ↔ annual replacement, accelerated sandbox renewal, refund/reversal and delayed webhook delivery. Calendar-month annual allowances need ledger tests as sandbox terms are accelerated.
- Customer aliases/transfers, concurrent cross-process refunds/grants and restore timeouts need staging exercises beyond current automated cases. A timed-out restore may finish in the SDK later; the UI does not treat that late completion as immediate success.
- Coverage is scoped: backend and selected mobile modules have enforced thresholds; percentages do not describe every screen or native path. Provider responses and store SDKs are mocked in most tests.
- Baseline JSX/React test-renderer peer warnings remain; they did not fail the suites. Expo prebuild warns about optional Android system UI and missing iOS team configuration. iOS signing is not configured by this work.
- The exact earlier conversational master checklist was unavailable. Reconcile it with this report if it contains requirements absent from the repository scope documents.
