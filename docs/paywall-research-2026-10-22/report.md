# Votic paywall research and implementation — October 22, 2026

## Outcome

Reviewed **76 distinct mobile subscription offers/paywalls** after screening 80 apps. Implemented improvements on `feature/paywall-research-improvements-2026-10-22`, based on audit commit `59cee87`. Main remains unchanged. The source sample supports design hypotheses; it does not establish Votic conversion lift.

The biggest opportunity is decision clarity: let users reach prices before a large demo, state that their own documents require a subscription, explain renewal beside checkout, and accurately represent eligible store offers. Keep Votic's existing voice preview, personalization, verified entitlements, restores and purchase celebration.

## Evidence and method

Access date for all examples: **2026-10-22, America/New_York**. Each counted example was visually inspected from a locally retrieved screenshot in [PaywallPro's Open Paywall Gallery](https://github.com/paywallpro/paywall-gallery). The inventory pins each case and image to the exact repository revision and records a screenshot SHA-256. The gallery's structured pricing/taxonomy was not treated as proof of what an image showed. App identity was cross-checked against its case page; the representative screenshot was read for layout, copy and controls.

**All 76 are archived third-party iOS screenshot observations. Zero are current firsthand installed-app observations.** Source case pages report version and version-release date; those are NOT capture dates. Capture dates are unavailable. Prices, availability, eligibility and layouts may have changed. The sample is a purposeful convenience sample, with substantial vendor, iOS, storefront and selection bias; it is not representative of all mobile paywalls. One analyst coded visibility; no inter-rater validation was performed.

The sample includes 34 Education, 18 Productivity, 7 Business, 3 Books, 3 Reference, 8 Health & Fitness, 2 Photo & Video and 1 Music apps. Directly relevant examples include Speechify, NaturalReader, Voice Dream, Peech, Blinkist, Headway, Imprint, Quizlet, Goodnotes, Adobe Acrobat, Evernote, WPS Office, HyNote, iTranscribe and document scanners.

Primary capture is `cover.webp`. Audible's `paywall-screen-3.webp` was inspected separately as a subscription promotion, not a paywall. Variants were inspected separately for ambiguous cases and are not counted as additional apps. Simply Piano's landscape cover was inspected individually. Audible's one-time audiobook purchase capture does not count as a subscription example.

Excluded after visual review: Acorn TV (entry/permission/offline screens, no plan-selection paywall verified), Pencil Bible (translation purchases without recurring terms), Udemy (individual course purchases; filenames claiming annual offers contradicted the actual screenshots). These four exclusions prevent a misleading count of 80 verified subscription paywalls. Audible is excluded from the paywall count because its inspected subscription promotion does not establish a plan-selection paywall.

## Separate evidence from experiments and primary documentation

1. [Mimo growth lead's RevenueCat account, May 19, 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/optimize-funnel-metrics-mimo): reports higher trial opt-in and trial-to-purchase after explaining the trial more transparently. The author also describes downstream plan-mix and retention effects. This is an operator-reported case, without enough public experimental detail to transfer an effect size to Votic. It supports testing clear terms and measuring beyond the first conversion.
2. [Paywall redesign case studies, RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/paywall-redesigns-case-studies): the designer reports improvements after shortening several paywalls, among other simultaneous changes. Anonymous apps, bundled interventions and unavailable assignment/sample details limit causal attribution. This motivates testing a shorter route to plans; it does not prove that moving plans alone increases sales. Trial toggles and price anchoring from these examples were not copied.
3. [Google Play subscription policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533): requires clear recurring prices, billing frequency, renewal/cancellation information and accurate access disclosures. An equivalent monthly price must not obscure an annual charge. This supports disclosure fixes independently of conversion hypotheses.
4. [RevenueCat offer and eligibility guidance](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/subscription-guidance/subscription-offers): iOS eligibility is checked through the SDK; Android uses eligible subscription options, with automatic offer selection when purchasing a package. Unknown iOS eligibility should not be presented as a verified trial. Expiry of a paid entitlement alone does not establish prior introductory-offer use.
5. [RevenueCat product display documentation](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/getting-started/displaying-products) and the installed `react-native-purchases` / purchases-typescript declarations: offer terms and localized prices come from current SDK product data. No competitor screenshot prices are used in the app.

## Prioritized gaps and changes

Expected benefits below are usability or accuracy expectations. Conversion effects remain unmeasured hypotheses.

| Priority | Before / gap | Evidence | Implemented change | Expected benefit; confidence; effort; risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Only free trials represented; eligible paid introductory phases could be omitted from billing copy | SDK pricing-phase model and offer docs | Carry localized paid introductory phases and payment counts through cards, checkout, CTA and terms; preserve regular renewal total | Accurate charge sequence; high technical confidence; medium effort; medium store edge-case risk pending sandbox |
| P0 | Any expired entitlement suppressed a trial even when store eligibility confirmed it | Expired is not prior-trial-use; RevenueCat eligibility guidance | Trust SDK eligibility rather than guessing from entitlement expiry; unknown iOS eligibility still hides introductions | Avoid contradictory terms; high confidence; low effort; medium risk requiring store checks |
| P0 | Generic errors claimed no charge without enough evidence | Generic SDK failure cannot establish settlement | Ask users to restore before retrying; remove unsupported no-charge assurances | Reduce misleading advice and duplicate attempts; high confidence; low effort; low risk |
| P1 | Substantial demo and benefits preceded plan selection | Visual audit; concise-design hypothesis from case studies | Move plans and billing explanation before full demo; retain interactive preview and all benefit expansion | Easier price discovery; medium conversion confidence; low effort; value-before-price sequencing needs measurement |
| P1 | Checkout summary omitted explicit renewal; cancellation instructions mostly in scroll/FAQ | Google policy; 56/76 show cancellation/renewal explanation | State auto-renewal next to CTA, name the store, provide direct store-management link with error feedback | Clearer commitment and exit route; high clarity confidence; low effort; low risk |
| P1 | Hard subscription requirement implicit; AI allowance only inside FAQ | Router and server code; accurate access requirements | State own documents require subscription, sample is free; show AI allowance notice in visible benefits | Set accurate expectations; high clarity confidence; low effort; low risk |
| P1 | Fixed footer could consume short screens or large-font viewport | Layout code inspection | Move the same checkout controls into scrolling flow for height under 600 or font scale at least 1.5 | Keep content and controls reachable; medium confidence until device checks; low effort; medium layout risk |

| P2 | Delayed offer eligibility result could outlive identity change | Account-aware service review | Discard offering results after identity generation changes before populating package cache | Avoid stale account offers; high confidence; low effort; low risk |

## Preserved and deliberately deferred

Preserved: main, subscription products/prices, dashboard settings, entitlements, production access rules, trial configuration, authentication, documents, finish experience, Notes, animations, native feedback, purchasing, restores, existing plan selection and personalized hero text. No retired Review or graded Check My Understanding screen was restored.

No fake urgency, fabricated proof, unverified popular-plan claims, forced trial toggles, hidden prices, new account requirement or new tracking. Actual cancellation still happens in the store. No trial-end reminder promise was added because Votic does not have a verified reminder-delivery system.

Business/configuration recommendations requiring a separate decision: whether to offer a free document experience, whether to change pricing or trial length, whether to fund reminder delivery, which plans/tier families to expose, and whether to introduce funnel analytics. Review RevenueCat/Play configuration for developer-determined offers, prepaid plans, installments and unusual pricing phases before release; these configurations cannot be verified from the repository. Current code assumes ordinary auto-renewing subscriptions. Do not infer correct setup from mocks.

The app currently uses English UI copy with localized store currency strings and locale-formatted estimates. Complete non-English billing-copy localization and right-to-left/device validation are not implemented by this change; prioritize them before offering unsupported language experiences. Savings compare regular same-currency annual and monthly prices, not introductory charges; configured product families must grant comparable benefits.

## Measurement plan

Use a stable randomized control/treatment assignment only after an existing approved analytics route or separately approved analytics setup is available. Keep product, price, eligibility and acquisition mix comparable. First verify event integrity and distinguish unique paywall viewers from repeated opens.

Proposed events: paywall viewed, plan selected, purchase started, purchase result (confirmed/cancelled/pending/failed), restore result and explicit exit/sign-out. Use an opaque visit/experiment identifier, variant, entry point, billing period and eligibility category. Do not include email, document text, receipt contents, payment tokens or card data. Track pending-to-confirmed transitions separately and deduplicate native callbacks.

Primary outcome: confirmed paid subscribers per unique eligible paywall viewer, separating immediate purchases from trials and allowing each trial cohort to mature. Diagnostic outcomes: offer-load success, plan-selection rate, purchase-start rate, store failures, trial starts and restore recovery. Guardrails: refunds, early cancellations, trial-to-paid rate, first and later renewals, paid retention, support contacts and post-purchase reading/listening activation. Monitor plan mix and net revenue per eligible viewer alongside conversion.

Set a minimum worthwhile effect and power/sample-size plan from actual baseline traffic; no invented sample size or calendar stopping date. Report uncertainty and cohort maturity. Do not declare a win on trial starts alone or repeatedly peek until a result looks positive. Votic conversion improvement has not been measured in this work.
