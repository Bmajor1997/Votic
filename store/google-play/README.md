# Google Play store images

Images for Votic's Play Store listing (Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Store listings → Default store listing).

| File | Where it goes | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `votic-icon-512.png` | App icon | 512 × 512 |
| `votic-feature-graphic-1024x500.png` | Feature graphic | 1024 × 500 |
| `votic-screenshot-1-read-or-listen.png` … `votic-screenshot-6-comfortable.png` | Phone screenshots, in number order | 1080 × 1920 |

The screenshots tell a short story, in order: read or listen your way, ask your documents, bring your documents together, save what matters, pick up where you left off, make reading comfortable. They were captured from the app on the Android emulator using public-domain sample documents (NASA's "Sun: Facts" and "Moon Facts", the U.S. Constitution and O. Henry's "The Gift of the Magi"), then framed with a caption on a light blue background. The listing text (name, short and full description) is in the "Votic Google Play Listing" doc.
